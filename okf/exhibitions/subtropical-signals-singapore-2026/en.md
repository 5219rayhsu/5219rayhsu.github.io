# Subtropical Signals: Contemporary Image-Making from Taiwan — DECK, Singapore Group Exhibition

HSU Che-jui's work featured in the group exhibition *Subtropical Signals: Contemporary Image-Making from Taiwan* (亞熱帶訊號：台灣當代影像生產), 2026/06/19–07/26 at Shop–House by DECK, Singapore, curated by Joanna Fu and organized by DECK Photography Art Centre, with an opening reception on 06/18. Featured *Chaos Body Shop* (Sutra Fold). Covered by China Times (Truemii).

## Key facts

- Date: 2026-06-19 – 2026-07-26 (opening reception: 2026-06-18, 19:00–21:00)
- Venue: Shop–House by DECK (4 Lorong 24 Geylang, Singapore 398616)
- Curator: Joanna Fu (傅爾得)
- Organizer: DECK Photography Art Centre
- Featured work: *Chaos Body Shop* (Sutra Fold)
