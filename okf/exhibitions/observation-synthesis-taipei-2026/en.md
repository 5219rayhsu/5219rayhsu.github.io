# Observation Synthesis — HyperWave Space Group Exhibition

HSU Che-jui's work featured in the group exhibition *Observation Synthesis* (觀和作用), 2026/07/08–07/25 at HyperWave Space, instructed by YAO Jui-chung and curated by Shino Huang and JAO Yi-chen, with two guest-speaker talks (07/18, 07/25). Featured *Answer* and *Chaos Body Shop* (Sutra Fold).

## Key facts

- Date: 2026-07-08 – 2026-07-25
- Venue: HyperWave Space (4F, No. 8, Sec. 1, Nanjing E. Rd., Zhongshan Dist., Taipei)
- Instructor: YAO, Jui-chung (姚瑞中)
- Curators: Shino Huang (黃筱芸), JAO, Yi-chen (饒逸蓁)
- Talks: 2026-07-18 (Sat) 15:00–17:00, guest speakers MA Li-chun and CHEN Chin-pao; 2026-07-25 (Sat) 15:00–17:00, guest speakers CHANG Wen-hao and LIN Chun-yeh
- Featured works: *Answer*, *Chaos Body Shop* (Sutra Fold)
- Official page: https://5219rayhsu.github.io/en/exhibitions/#observation-synthesis-2026
